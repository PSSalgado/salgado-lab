### About Me

Anna was a postdoctoral researcher in the Salgado Lab (from 2017), working on the structure and function of proteins involved in *C. difficile* pathobiology. She contributed to characterisation of the S-layer and its links with infectivity, using microbiology, biochemistry, biophysics and structural biology.

She was awarded a BBSRC DTP PhD in 2018 for work on *S. aureus* superoxide dismutases with Dr Kevin Waldron at Newcastle University. In early 2026 she moved on to new professional adventures beyond the Salgado Lab.
