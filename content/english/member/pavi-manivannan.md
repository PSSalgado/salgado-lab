### About Me

Pavi (Pavithra Manivannan) is a BBSRC NEEDL PhD student in the Salgado Lab. Her research focuses on cell wall proteins and the S-layer of *C. difficile*, combining biochemistry, structural approaches and imaging to understand how surface proteins are organised and function.
