### About Me

Victoria completed her PhD in Molecular Sciences for Medicine (MoSMed CDT, 2021–2025) in the Salgado Lab. Her project explored the functionality of *C. difficile* engulfasome proteins, including fragment-based approaches aimed at preventing sporulation and transmission.

In her spare time, she loves walking around the hidden gems of the North-East and the Lake District.
